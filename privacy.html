Burning the City

Privacy Policy & Support · Last updated: October 20, 2026

Support
Questions, bug reports or privacy requests? Write to sanadservicebooking@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

1. Who we are

Burning the City is a free 3D arcade game published by Sanad Island (Sanad El Bachari), the developer. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer of the game.

2. What the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, diamonds, unlocked items and settings) is saved locally on your device. The game has no account, no login and no server of ours that stores your gameplay data.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The game uses two third-party services that may collect information automatically when you play:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — to show banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and operating-system information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and information about how you interact with ads.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — to understand how the game is used and to improve it. It may collect an app-instance identifier, device model, operating-system version, country, session length and in-game events such as "level started", "level completed" or "level failed".

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or payment information.

4. How we use this information

  • To show ads, which keeps the game free;
  • To measure and improve the game (for example, to find levels that are too hard);
  • To keep the game working and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising partners such as Google may use the data described above to serve and measure ads, under their own privacy policies.

5. Your choices

  • iPhone / iPad: the game asks for your permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can refuse, or change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can still play if you refuse.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: the game shows a consent message (Google's consent platform) the first time you play, where you can accept or refuse personalized ads and change your choice later.
  • You can also uninstall the game at any time, which removes the data stored on your device.

6. Children

Burning the City is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help.

7. Data retention and your rights

We do not keep personal data ourselves. Data held by Google (AdMob and Firebase Analytics) is retained according to Google's policies. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address above, or use the controls in your device settings.
